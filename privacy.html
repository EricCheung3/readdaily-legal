Privacy Policy — ReadDaily

Last updated: 2026-10-05

ReadDaily ("we", "our") is an English learning app that turns daily real-world news into intensive-reading lessons. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Data stored only on your device

The following never leaves your phone:

  • Check-in dates, streaks, freeze tokens, milestones
  • Your word bank, lesson progress, and shadowing completion records
  • App install date, free-trial status, AI scoring quota usage
  • Referral code state and promotional Pro grants

2. Data sent to our servers

  • **Voice recordings for shadowing scores.** When you tap "Score", your recording is uploaded solely to transcribe it into text. The audio file is deleted from our server immediately after transcription. We do not keep your recordings.
  • **Referral redemptions.** When you enter a friend's invite code, we store a random install ID, the code, and the redemption time — only to grant both sides the referral reward. No name, email, or phone number is collected; there is no account system.

3. Third-party services

  • **Speech transcription** (OpenAI or Groq): receives your recording audio and returns a text transcript, per their privacy policies.
  • **AI feedback** (DeepSeek): receives the practice sentence and its transcript to generate a short Chinese pronunciation tip.
  • **Subscriptions** (RevenueCat): processes App Store / Google Play subscription status.
  • **Ads** (Google AdMob): we show only rewarded video ads (watch ~30 seconds to unlock the day). AdMob may collect advertising identifiers per Google's policy. Subscribers see no ads.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Permissions we request

  • **Microphone** — only for shadowing (read-aloud) practice, only while you are recording.
  • **Notifications** — optional daily 8:00 AM learning reminder; you can turn it off anytime.

5. Children

ReadDaily is rated 4+ / Everyone. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Data retention & deletion

Voice recordings are deleted right after scoring. Referral records are kept only as long as needed for rewards. Because we hold no account data, there is nothing to export — to wipe everything, delete the app.

7. Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes. Continued use after changes means you accept the update.
